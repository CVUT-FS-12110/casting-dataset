# Common Cast-Iron Casting Categories

## Goal
This document summarizes the most common categories of cast-iron castings used in industry.  
The categories are organized according to their approximate production prevalence and industrial importance.

---

# 🚗 Automotive Components

- ✅ [brake discs](categories/brake_discs.md)
- ❌ brake drums
- ❌ engine blocks
- ❌ exhaust manifolds
- ❌ transmission housings
- ❌ differential housings
- ❌ wheel hubs
- ❌ suspension components

### Notes
- Largest production share overall
- Extremely high-volume manufacturing
- Dominated by gray and ductile iron castings

---

# 🚰 Pipes and Infrastructure Components

- ❌ sewer covers
- ❌ water supply pipes
- ❌ ductile iron pipelines
- ❌ valves
- ❌ gate valves
- ✅ [threaded pipe fittings](categories/threaded_pipe_fittings.md)
- ❌ hydrant bodies

### Notes
- Long-term infrastructure demand
- Large-scale municipal production
- Ductile iron usage is increasing

---

# 🏭 Machine Frames and Structural Castings

- ❌ machine tool beds
- ❌ machine frames
- ❌ industrial bases
- ❌ support structures
- ❌ gearbox housings
- ❌ structural supports

### Notes
- Lower production count than automotive
- Very large individual casting masses
- High dimensional stability requirements

---

# ⚙️ Pump, Compressor and Industrial Equipment Housings

- ❌ pump housings
- ❌ compressor housings
- ❌ industrial casings
- ❌ blower housings
- ❌ valve bodies
- ❌ fluid handling components

### Notes
- Common in industrial systems
- Medium production volumes
- Complex internal geometries

---

# 🔄 Rotating and Friction Components

- ❌ flywheels
- ❌ pulleys
- ❌ friction rings
- ❌ clutch components
- ❌ rotating discs
- ❌ balancing wheels

### Notes
- Often require good wear resistance
- Rotational symmetry common
- Frequently machined after casting

---

# 🔥 Energy and Heating Components

- ❌ radiators
- ❌ boiler bodies
- ❌ furnace components
- ❌ burner housings
- ❌ energy system components
- ❌ heat exchanger parts

### Notes
- Historically very common
- Some decline due to material substitution
- High thermal resistance requirements

---

# 🧱 Miscellaneous Castings

- ❌ counterweights
- ❌ gears
- ❌ decorative castings
- ❌ benches
- ❌ fences
- ❌ ornamental castings
- ❌ industrial ballast parts

### Notes
- Broad category with diverse applications
- Includes both industrial and decorative products

---

# Important Remarks

- Highest production quantity does not necessarily mean highest mass.
- Machine frames are often extremely heavy but produced in small series.
- Automotive components dominate total production count.
- Ductile iron usage continues to grow, especially in:
  - infrastructure,
  - pipelines,
  - automotive applications.
