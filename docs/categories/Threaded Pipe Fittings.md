# Threaded Pipe Fittings Dataset (R 1")

**Size:** R 1" (DN25)
**Standards:** EN 10241 (steel threaded pipe fittings), ISO 7-1 / EN 10226-1 (BSPT threads)
**Thread:** BSPT R1, 11 TPI, taper 1:16, major diameter 33.249 mm

**Reference Catalogs**

* https://www.kompresory-vzduchotechnika.cz/zavitova-sroubeni-ra--z-pozinkovane-oceli/
* https://hermannschmidt.de/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Stahlfittings_2021_ohne_Preise.pdf

## 1. Nipple

**Description:** R1 male-to-male threaded pipe fitting for joining two female threaded ports.

**Approximate dimensions**

* Overall length: 65 mm
* Outer diameter: Ø39.5 mm
* Through bore: Ø27 mm
* Hex section: 46 mm AF × 25 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Create male threaded sections on both ends.
2. Add a central hex wrench section.
3. Cut a continuous through bore.
4. Add continuous external helical thread ridges on both male ends.

## 2. Hex Coupling

**Description:** R1 hexagonal female-to-female threaded pipe fitting with wrench flats.

**Approximate dimensions**

* Overall length: 50 mm
* Central hex body: 46 mm AF
* Through bore: Ø31 mm
* Female socket lip radius: R1.5 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Create a central hexagonal prism 46 mm AF.
2. Drill through bore Ø31 mm.
3. Add internal R1 BSPT threads from both ends to 22 mm depth.
4. Apply an approximately 1.5 mm outward-facing fillet/full-round edge to the outer perimeter of each female socket opening.
5. Add lead-in chamfers.

## 4. Reducer

**Description:** R1 reducer fitting with an R1 male side and a smaller R3/4 female side.

**Configuration**

* R1 male × R3/4 female

**Approximate dimensions**

* Overall length: 40 mm
* Large body diameter: 46 mm
* Male thread length: 20 mm
* Female thread depth: 18 mm
* Female socket lip radius: R1.5 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Create a male threaded section running cleanly from the free end to the hex body.
2. Add R1 male thread (20 mm).
3. Add a thinner female-side tube with R3/4 female thread (18 mm depth).
4. Keep the female socket connected to the main tube body.
5. Apply an approximately 1.5 mm outward-facing fillet/full-round edge to the outer perimeter of the female socket opening.
6. Add wrench flats (46 mm AF).

## 7. Bend 90 FF

**Description:** R1 female-to-female long bend for turning a pipe run through 90 degrees.

**Approximate dimensions**

* Center-to-end: 45 mm
* Thin body diameter: Ø36 mm
* Female socket diameter: Ø42 mm
* Bore: Ø27 mm
* Female socket lip radius: R1.5 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Sweep a thin Ø36 mm body through a smooth 90° arc with straight socket tails.
2. Create internal flow passage Ø27 mm.
3. Thicken only the female socket ends to Ø42 mm and blend them back to the thin body.
4. Add internal R1 BSPT threads (22 mm depth) at both female ends on the local tube axis.
5. Apply an approximately 1.5 mm outward-facing fillet/full-round edge to the outer perimeter of each female socket opening.
6. Add wrench flats if required.

## 13. Tee

**Description:** R1 three-port threaded pipe fitting for branching a fluid line at right angles.

**Approximate dimensions**

* Main run center-to-end: 50 mm
* Branch center-to-end: 50 mm
* Thin body diameter: Ø36 mm
* Female socket diameter: Ø42 mm
* Bore: Ø27 mm
* Female socket lip radius: R1.5 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Create a horizontal run and one perpendicular branch.
2. Close the unused fourth shoulder so the part is a true tee, not a cross.
3. Blend intersections smoothly.
4. Thicken only the three female socket ends and blend them back to the thinner body.
5. Add female R1 BSPT threads on all three ports.
6. Apply approximately 1.5 mm outward-facing fillets/full-round edges to the female socket openings.

---

## 14. Reducing Tee

**Description:** R1 threaded branch fitting with a smaller R3/4 branch port.

**Configuration**

* R1 × R1 × R3/4

**Approximate dimensions**

* Same as Tee
* Reduced branch bore: Ø24 mm
* R1 female socket diameter: Ø42 mm
* R3/4 female socket diameter: Ø36 mm
* Female socket lip radius: R1.5 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Start from Tee geometry.
2. Reduce branch size to R3/4.
3. Close the unused fourth shoulder so the part is a true reducing tee, not a cross.
4. Thicken only the three female socket ends and blend them back to the thinner body.
5. Add corresponding internal threads.
6. Apply approximately 1.5 mm outward-facing fillets/full-round edges to the female socket openings.

## 15. Cross

**Description:** R1 four-port threaded pipe fitting for distributing a line in two perpendicular directions.

**Approximate dimensions**

* Center-to-end: 50 mm
* Thin body diameter: Ø36 mm
* Female socket diameter: Ø42 mm
* Bore: Ø27 mm
* Female socket lip radius: R1.5 mm

**Modeling procedure**

1. Create four intersecting cylindrical branches.
2. Blend all intersections.
3. Thicken only the four female socket ends and blend them back to the thinner body.
4. Add female R1 BSPT threads on all ports.
5. Apply approximately 1.5 mm outward-facing fillets/full-round edges to the female socket openings.


## General Modeling Notes

* Use BSPT R1 threads (ISO 7-1):

  * Major diameter: 33.249 mm
  * 11 TPI
  * Taper: 1:16

* Apply:

  * 1 mm × 45° chamfers at thread starts
  * R0.5–1 mm fillets on external edges

* Material:

  * Galvanized steel
  * Typical finish: hot-dip zinc coating

These dimensions are intended to closely follow common EN 10241 industrial fittings and provide a representative engineering dataset.
